The Methodology

We leverage technology, data, and advanced manual techniques to optimize your kinetic chain.

Biomechanics Lab

We don't guess, we measure. Utilizing high-speed 3D motion capture and dual force plates to analyze your kinetic chain.

  • 3D Gait & Sprint Analysis
  • Asymmetry Detection
  • Ground Reaction Force Testing
  • Power Output Optimization

Precision Needling

Intramuscular stimulation targeting myofascial trigger points to reset the neuromuscular system and accelerate healing.

  • Pain Modulation via CNS
  • Improved Tissue Extensibility
  • Accelerated Blood Flow
  • Post-Op Scar Tissue Breakdown

Manual & Strength

Combining hands-on joint mobilization with heavy, progressive resistance training to rebuild structural integrity.

  • Grade V Joint Manipulations
  • Hypertrophy & Tissue Loading
  • Blood Flow Restriction (BFR)
  • Sport-Specific Periodization

Return to Sport Timeline

Our criteria-based, rather than time-based, approach to getting you back on the field safely.

01

Pain & Inflammation

Controlling swelling, restoring basic range of motion, and activating inhibited musculature using manual therapy and modalities.

02

Strength & Capacity

Progressive overload. We rebuild foundational strength through heavy resistance training to ensure tissues can handle load.

03

Power & Plyometrics

Transitioning strength into speed. Reintroducing jumping, landing mechanics, and rate of force development (RFD).

04

Chaos & Agility

Unplanned, reactive movements replicating sport-specific scenarios. Cleared only when testing shows >95% symmetry.

Patient FAQ

Your initial 60-minute evaluation involves a deep dive into your injury history, goals, and a comprehensive biomechanical movement screen. Depending on your needs, this may include force plate testing, gait analysis, and preliminary manual therapy. We finish by building a roadmap for your complete return to sport.
Most patients report little to no pain during needle insertion. When the needle hits a trigger point, you may experience a brief "twitch response," which feels like a quick muscle cramp. This is the desired neurophysiological response that resets the muscle and alleviates pain.
We operate as an out-of-network, cash-based clinic. This model allows us to spend full 60-minute sessions one-on-one with you, utilizing advanced tech without insurance constraints dictating your care. We provide Superbills upon request for you to submit to your insurance for out-of-network reimbursement.
Timeline varies wildly based on the injury (e.g., an ankle sprain vs. an ACL reconstruction). However, our protocol is strictly criteria-based, not time-based. You progress when your metrics (strength, power, LSI) dictate you are ready, ensuring the absolute lowest risk of re-injury.
