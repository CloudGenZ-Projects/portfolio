A note from the farm
People and Provenance

Dear guest,

Every growing season teaches us to pay attention. To the soil after rain. To the first tender leaves in spring. To the fruit that needs another week, and the vegetables that are ready today whether or not we planned for them.

Our work begins long before a plate is served. We grow, gather, and select with the season in mind, accepting that nature does not follow a calendar printed in ink.

Working closely with the kitchen gives each harvest purpose. What comes out of the ground shapes what goes onto your plate, and what is flourishing today inspires tomorrow's service.

We hope you taste that sense of place in every course: not just in the ingredients, but in the quiet care that brought them here.

Julian & Clara Thorne
Field Stewards & Farm Founders
High Meadow Farm
Living Cultivation Practices: No chemical insecticides or synthetic stimulants. Living soil nurtured with green manure, composted vine trimmings, and rainwater swales.

Cellar & Barrel

Good wine doesn't compete with the meal. It completes the conversation.

Our cellar centers on low-intervention, character-rich vintages from growers who treat their vineyards with the same patient reverence we give our fields.
