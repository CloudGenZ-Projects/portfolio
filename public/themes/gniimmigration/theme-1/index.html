How Our Process Works

A structured 4-step framework engineered to eliminate application delays and rejection risks.

1

Initial Assessment

We evaluate your qualifications, ECA educational credentials, language test benchmarks, and CRS score against real-time IRCC requirements.

2

Document Preparation

Our case managers provide customized checklists, review employment verification reference letters, and ensure flawless formatting.

3

Application Support

We submit your formal application via the authorized CICC representative portal, accompanied by an extensive statutory submission letter.

4

Progress Updates

We handle all IRCC correspondence, biometrics booking, medical request coordination, through to your Confirmation of Permanent Residence (COPR).
