REPAIR / 001 - SYSTEM ACTIVE

The damage you see isn't always the damage that matters.

Chapter 01 / What you see

A damaged panel is only part of the picture.

A dent, a misaligned hood, or a cracked bumper can be obvious. What isn't obvious is whether surrounding components have shifted.

Chapter 02 / What we investigate

Look beyond the surface.

Our assessment examines visible damage and identifies areas requiring structural measurement or manufacturer-specific diagnostics.

Chapter 03 / The repair plan

Measure first. Repair with purpose.

When structural damage is suspected, measuring equipment and OEM repair information help establish the correct sequence of repairs.

Our Approach / Three Precision Systems

Engineered back to specification.

Structural integrity, panel fit, and finish quality are different parts of the same repair. We bring them together through a documented process.

01 / Structure

Laser frame straightening & measurement

A collision can affect structural reference points. Appropriate measurement helps technicians determine whether the structure is within tolerances.

  • Initial structural assessment
  • Electronic or laser-assisted measurement
  • Repair planning using OEM procedures
02 / Refinishing

Paint matching beyond the paint code

Lighting, age, finish type, and existing paint variation can affect how a color looks. We match the vehicle, not just the code.

  • Spectrophotometer-assisted analysis
  • Spray-out testing and visual comparison
  • Appropriate blending & preparation
03 / Bodywork

Panel fit. Surface quality. Finish.

From damaged panels to component replacement, every repair begins with an assessment of what can be repaired safely.

  • Panel repair or replacement
  • Gap and alignment checks
  • Reassembly and final inspection
