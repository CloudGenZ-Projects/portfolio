Meet Our Instructors

Touring choreographers, former company soloists, and passionate dance educators.

Ballet Artistic Director

Former soloist with the Paris Opera Ballet. Camille brings 15 years of technical rigor, artistic grace, and anatomical injury prevention.

Kevante Brooks

Head of Commercial & Hip-Hop

Contemporary & Improvisation

Juilliard alumna whose choreography has been staged at festivals across Europe and North America. Dedicated to expressive emotional authenticity.
