Onsite Imaging · Clinician-Guided

Less uncertainty. A clearer next step.

When an injury or symptom may require imaging, having digital X-ray equipment onsite makes the evaluation process more convenient. Your clinician will determine whether an X-ray is appropriate.

1

Clinical assessment

A clinician evaluates your symptoms, history and examination findings.

2

Imaging when appropriate

If indicated and available, the team arranges an X-ray of the relevant area.

3

Results and next steps

A qualified clinician reviews the available findings and explains the treatment or referral plan.

*Onsite digital X-ray is designed to support clinical evaluation. Not every concern requires imaging, and an X-ray cannot detect every type of injury. Some conditions require additional testing at a hospital.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an appointment?
This clinic is presented as a walk-in facility, but please confirm our current walk-in policy and hours before travelling. Some specific services may require additional arrangements.
Can I get an X-ray onsite?
Yes, we have digital X-ray equipment onsite. A qualified clinician will evaluate your condition first to determine if imaging is medically appropriate for your specific injury or symptom.
How long will my visit take?
Visit times vary based on the number of patients currently in the clinic and the complexity of your evaluation. We prioritize severe cases, and will do our best to evaluate you efficiently.
What conditions can urgent care treat?
We treat non-life-threatening illnesses and injuries, including minor cuts, sprains, common infections, and cold/flu symptoms. We do not treat severe emergencies like chest pain or major trauma.
What should I bring?
Please bring a valid photo identification, your insurance details (if applicable), a list of current medications, relevant allergy information, and any useful medical records.
