Frequently Asked Questions

Ensuring your air conditioning system is best for your home. Our FrostFree Consultation service provides expert guidance tailored to your specific cooling capacity needs.

What should I do in an electrical HVAC emergency?

Signs that you may need to check your system's wiring include frequent breaker trips when the AC kicks on, burning smells from the vents, or a condenser fan that refuses to spin. Shut off power at the breaker panel immediately and call our emergency line.

What are the signs that my AC needs refrigerant?

If your AC is running continuously but the air coming from vents isn't cold, or if you see ice building up on the evaporator coil, you likely have a refrigerant leak. Do not add freon without fixing the leak first.

How often should I replace my air filters?

For optimal airflow and air quality, standard 1-inch filters should be replaced every 30 to 90 days. If you have pets, allergies, or a highly active home, replacing them monthly is highly recommended to prevent compressor strain.
