Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What forms of identification are legally acceptable?
State laws require unexpired government-issued identification containing a photograph, signature, physical description, and serial number. Accepted IDs include state driver’s licenses, state ID cards, US passports, foreign passports stamped by USCIS, and US military identification.
Can a notary draft or provide legal advice on my document?
No. A Notary Public is not an attorney licensed to practice law and cannot give legal advice, prepare legal documents, or advise you on which form to sign. If you have questions about legal rights or consequences, please consult an attorney.
How fast can a mobile notary arrive for an emergency signing?
For urgent requests (hospital bedside, jail visitation, or last-minute flight departures), we can usually dispatch an agent to arrive within 45 to 60 minutes across our primary metro territory.
What if a signer does not possess a valid government photo ID?
Under state notary law, identity can often be established through one or two credible witnesses who personally know the signer, have valid photo ID, and are impartial third parties with no financial interest in the document.
