“ A Different Kind of Evening

Not simply a meal. A moment that stays with you long after the candles burn low.

There is an unmistakable luxury in letting an evening unfold without hurry. The quiet clinking of stemware. The aroma of ember-roasted herbs and caramelized fats. Dishes tuned precisely to their season. At Ember & Vine, we bring these rituals together with quiet confidence. Come for dinner. Stay for the evening.

From The Kitchen

Good food begins long before the flame.

Our kitchen begins with a simple question: what is worth cooking right now? We build each menu around produce at its absolute peak, using smoke, hearth ember, and patience as tools of refinement rather than theatrics.

01 — The Harvest

Ingredients with a sense of place.

We partner with regenerative growers and coastal purveyors who care deeply about their craft, allowing the harvest to dictate the menu.

02 — The Fire

Technique with restraint.

Char, smoke, and slow heat deepen flavors and accentuate natural sweetness without masking pristine ingredients.

03 — The Finish

Every detail earns its place.

From the tactile weight of artisan ceramics to house-churned cultured butters, nothing unnecessary is added.

“Cooking is a quiet conversation between the harvest, the season, and the hearth. Our responsibility is to listen attentively and intervene with care.”

— Executive Chef & Culinary Director
