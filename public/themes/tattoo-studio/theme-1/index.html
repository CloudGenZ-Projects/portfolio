Clinical Hygiene & Safety

Your physical safety is our primary ethical duty. We maintain protocols rivaling minor surgical operatories.

100% Single-Use Needles

Every cartridge needle is sterile blister-packed and unsealed exclusively in front of your eyes before disposal into medical bio-hazard sharps bins.

Hospital-Grade Sterilization

Non-disposable steel furniture is processed via Class-B Autoclaves under high steam pressure and vacuum dried with quarterly spore validation.

Medical Nitrile PPE

Zero powdered latex. Our artists employ hypoallergenic medical nitrile gloves, barrier clip sleeves, and machine baggies replaced for every phase.

EU Certified Safe Inks

We exclusively dispense REACH-compliant, gamma-sterilized vegan pigments from world standard labs (Dynamic, Eternal, Silverback).

HEPA H13 Air Filtration

Continuous medical air purifiers scrub airborne aerosols, maintaining an ultra-low particulate count within all tattooing rooms.

Cross-Contamination Shields

All contact surfaces, lamp switches, and power supplies are double-wrapped with medical film and disinfected with hospital virucides.

Healing Roadmap

The Healing Phases

A stunning tattoo is 50% artist execution and 50% post-session discipline. Follow this phase-by-phase protocol.

Phase 1: First 24 Hours (Dermal Shield & Rest)

Your tattoo is an open abrasion weeping plasma. The medical film applied by our artists acts as an artificial epidermis protecting against bacteria.

Critical Protocols (DO)

  • Leave the protective second-skin bandage intact for 24 hours minimum.
  • If blood/plasma pools under the film, this is completely normal and accelerates cellular healing.
  • Rest the tattooed limb, hydrate with water, and avoid strenuous workouts.
  • Sleep in clean, loose, breathable dark cotton clothing.

Strictly Forbidden (AVOID)

  • DO NOT remove the bandage prematurely with unwashed hands.
  • DO NOT submerge in hot baths, saunas, swimming pools, or ocean water.
  • DO NOT consume excessive alcohol which thins blood and increases weeping.
  • DO NOT allow pets to lick or sleep directly beside the fresh ink.

Phase 2: Days 2 – 7 (Washing & Hydration)

Peel the dermal bandage slowly under lukewarm running water. The skin will begin mild flaking and peeling resembling a sunburn.

Critical Protocols (DO)

  • Wash 2x daily using mild fragrance-free antibacterial liquid soap.
  • Pat dry gently with clean disposable paper towels (never abrasive bath towels).
  • Apply an ultra-thin layer of unscented ointment (Aquaphor or studio aftercare balm).
  • Let the skin breathe—less ointment is better than smothering the pores.

Strictly Forbidden (AVOID)

  • NEVER pick, scratch, or peel flaking scabs; you will pull pigment out.
  • Avoid tight athletic gym clothes that create friction over the tattoo.
  • Avoid direct, harsh sunlight exposure completely.
  • Do not apply thick Petroleum Jelly (Vaseline) which blocks oxygenation.

Phase 3: Weeks 2 – 4 (Long-Term Preservation)

Surface peeling is finished, but the deep dermis is actively locking the pigments down for the coming decades.

Critical Protocols (DO)

  • Switch to gentle unscented daily body lotion to prevent dry epidermis.
  • Apply broad spectrum SPF 50+ sunscreen anytime the tattoo is outdoors.
  • Schedule your complimentary 6-week healed photo check-in at our studio.

Strictly Forbidden (AVOID)

  • Do not expose unprotected healed ink to UV tanning beds.
  • Avoid harsh chemical exfoliants, scrubs, or retinol directly over the tattoo.

Clarity & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about pain, deposits, healing time, cover-ups, and studio etiquette.

Tattoo pain varies significantly depending on placement. Fleshy areas like outer biceps, forearms, calves, and outer thighs feel like a constant scratching vibration (pain rating 2 to 4 out of 10). Bony or nerve-dense areas like ribs, spine, sternum, wrists, and feet are notably more sensitive (pain rating 7 to 8 out of 10). Our artists use specialized gentle tapering techniques and take breaks whenever required.
You must be strictly 18 years or older. We verify official government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport, Driver's License) before any consultation or needle unsealing. No exceptions are made even with parental consent.
Minimalist and small fine-line tattoos typically take 30 to 60 minutes. Custom palm-sized pieces take 2.5 to 4 hours. Full-day projects (such as large forearm pieces or half-sleeves) require 6 to 8 hours. Full sleeves and back-pieces are scheduled over 4 to 8 separate day sessions spaced 3 weeks apart for proper skin healing.
Price is calculated based on four objective elements: (1) Total square surface area (dimensions in inches), (2) Stencil detail complexity (single-line vs hyper-realism), (3) Body placement (ribs and neck take longer due to skin elasticity), and (4) Artist tenure. Small tattoos start at ₹1,500; bespoke pieces average ₹3,000 to ₹12,000+.
Yes. A modest deposit of ₹1,000 (for smaller tattoos) or ₹3,000 (for large full-day sessions) is required to lock your appointment slot on the artist's calendar. This deposit is non-refundable but 100% deductible from your final session bill. You can reschedule once with at least 48 hours notice.
We welcome walk-ins every day based on resident artist availability between 12:00 PM and 7:00 PM for flash and smaller designs. However, for custom realism, portraits, and large sleeves, pre-booking with an advance consultation is mandatory to allow our artists sufficient drafting time.
Yes, 100%. Our artists are certified cover-up specialists. We assess the age, pigment saturation, and scarring of your existing ink. Depending on darkness, we either design an intentional organic dark-flow motif (Japanese Irezumi or dark floral geometry) or recommend 1-2 light sessions of laser fading prior to covering.
Surface epidermis healing takes approximately 10 to 14 days, during which peeling and light flaking complete. However, deeper dermal healing—where your white blood cells fully encapsulate the pigment particles—takes approximately 4 to 6 weeks. Following our aftercare guidelines guarantees vibrant longevity.
