Frequently Asked Questions

What does turnkey renovation mean?
Turnkey renovation typically means a coordinated process covering agreed planning, construction, trade coordination, finishing, and handover. The exact scope should be documented in your contract.
How much does a luxury renovation cost?
Costs vary significantly based on scope, materials, and existing conditions. During our discovery and estimation phases, we provide detailed pricing tailored to your specific project requirements.
How long will my renovation take?
Timelines depend on the complexity and scale of the project, as well as permitting schedules. A firm schedule is provided once the design and scope are finalized.
Do I need permits for a home addition?
Yes. As a full-service design-build firm, we handle the preparation, submission, and management of all necessary architectural and construction permits required by local municipalities.
