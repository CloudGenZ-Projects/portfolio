The Fermentation Craft

The 60-Hour Transformation

True bread is not made by rushing time. Our loaves rest in French wicker bannetons inside chilled cellars to develop deep lactic sweetness and gentle digestibility.

Hour 01 - 04

Autolyse & Stoneground Milling

French organic wheat flours absorb spring water, triggering natural enzymatic breakdowns that produce effortless hydration.

Hour 04 - 10

Rhythmic Stretch & Fold Aeration

Hand-stretched at 45-minute intervals inside wooden troughs to build supple gluten webbing without mechanical bruising.

Hour 10 - 58

Cold Fermentation Retardation

Slow maturation at 6°C allows wild lactobacilli to metabolize sugars, releasing aromatic esters and breaking down complex proteins.

Hour 60

Cast-Iron Deck Hearth Fire

Scored with a razor lamé and steam-baked on high-heat refractory stone decks for that singing, crackling crust.

Our Heritage

Born from the Paris Hearth, Rooted in Patience

“Bread is living poetry. It breathes the ambient morning fog, listens to the wood deck, and requires nothing more than water, flour, salt, and devotion.”

Founded by fourth-generation boulanger Henri Levain, our bakery began in a small stone alleyway along the Canal Saint-Martin. We preserve the ancestral methods of long fermentation, refusing the shortcuts of industrial commercial additives.

Stoneground Heritage Flours

Milled slowly between cold stones to preserve wheat germ vitality, natural oils, and mineral density.

Living Fermentation

Our sourdough mother starter is fed twice daily at dawn and dusk with organic whole rye and alpine water.
