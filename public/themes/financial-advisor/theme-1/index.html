Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent answers regarding our fiduciary standard, fee structure, and client relationships.

As a Registered Investment Advisor (RIA), we are legally bound to the fiduciary standard. This means we are required by law to put your interests ahead of our own at all times. We do not sell proprietary products, accept third-party commissions, or earn referral kickbacks.
We charge a transparent, tiered percentage fee based on Assets Under Management (AUM), starting at 0.85% annually and scaling down as your portfolio grows. There are no hidden trading charges or termination fees.
Our private wealth practice typically works with individuals and families who have $500,000 or more in investable assets. We also offer specialized fixed-fee financial planning arrangements for emerging high-income professionals.
Your assets are held in your name at Charles Schwab or Fidelity Institutional. WealthVista has limited trading authority to manage your portfolio according to your approved investment policy statement, but cannot withdraw funds.
