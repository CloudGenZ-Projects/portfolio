04 · Safety Education

Keep Your Garage Safe for Your Family.

Safety Sensor Awareness

Keep infrared eyes clean and aligned 6" above the floor.

Automatic Reversal

Door should reverse immediately when contacting a 2x4 block.

Balance Warning Signs

If a heavy door slams shut, stop using it — spring tension may be failing.

Child & Pet Safety

Mount wall buttons at least 5 ft high away from children.
