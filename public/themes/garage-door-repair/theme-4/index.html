Homeowner Safety

Garage Door Safety Center

Photo-Eye Sensors

Keep infrared lenses clean and unobstructed; doors must reverse if the beam is broken.

Auto-Reverse Test

Place a solid 2x4 flat on the floor; a calibrated opener reverses upon light contact.

Balance & Springs

A heavy or slamming door signals spring fatigue — never adjust high-tension springs yourself.

Child & Pet Safety

Mount wall controls at least 5 ft high and keep fingers clear of panel hinges.

Sound Identification

What Is Your Garage Door Trying to Tell You?
