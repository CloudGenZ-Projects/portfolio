Year-Round Readiness

Seasonal Garage Door Care

Protect moving hardware and weather seals through every seasonal temperature shift.

Spring

Inspect bottom weather stripping after winter, clear track debris, and test safety reversal sensors.

Summer

Check opener battery backup health, tighten vibration-loosened hinge bolts, and lubricate steel bearings.

Fall

Schedule a pre-winter balance and seal inspection to prevent drafts and cold-weather strain on springs.

Winter

Keep snow clear of the bottom seal to prevent freezing, and listen for stiff cold-start opener labor.
