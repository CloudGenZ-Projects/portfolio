Our Method · No Guesswork

Every repair has a reason.

A good repair starts long before a wrench turns. It starts with listening, testing and explaining what the evidence tells us.

01

Listen before we diagnose

We ask when the issue started, how the vehicle behaves and what you've already noticed.

02

Test, inspect and verify

We combine relevant scans, visual inspections and tests instead of replacing parts based on assumptions.

03

Explain the options

We discuss the findings, priorities, costs and recommended next steps in plain language.

04

Repair and quality-check

After authorization, we perform the agreed work and carry out appropriate post-repair checks.

Common Questions

How does a diagnostic appointment work? +
We start by discussing your symptoms, then perform system scans and visual inspections. We compile a report of our findings and discuss the necessary repair options with you before proceeding.
Is the diagnostic fee separate from the repair cost? +
Yes, the diagnostic fee covers the technician's time and equipment usage to accurately find the problem. Repair costs (parts and labor) are estimated separately once the issue is identified.
Will you explain the repair before starting? +
Absolutely. We never start a repair without your explicit authorization. You will receive a clear estimate explaining what needs fixing and why.
What warranty applies to parts and labor? +
We offer a 24-month / 24,000-mile warranty on all our standard parts and labor, ensuring your peace of mind long after you leave the shop.
