Chapter 01 • Psychoeducation

Thoughts Are Not Facts

The human brain is a survival prediction engine. It manufactures catastrophic narratives not to torment you, but out of an evolutionary reflex to protect you from uncertainty.

"Notice the thought: 'I am having the thought that I am failing' instead of 'I am failing'."
