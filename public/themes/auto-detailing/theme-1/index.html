Division 01 / Collision & Body Engineering

The real repair begins beneath the surface.

A vehicle can look straight while concealed damage remains. After a collision, the repair process needs to consider the vehicle's structure, manufacturer repair procedures, safety systems and the condition of the panels and finish.

Our process begins with a thorough assessment, followed by appropriate structural measurements and a repair plan tailored to the vehicle and the damage.

OEM-Informed Frame Measurement

Assess structural reference points using suitable measurement equipment and vehicle-specific specifications.

Panel Repair and Fitment

Evaluate whether a panel can be repaired or requires replacement. Check panel gaps, contours, mounting points and fitment before refinishing.

Color Matching and Refinishing

Identify the paint system and color code, evaluate the existing finish, prepare the surface and use appropriate mixing and blending procedures.

Division 02 / Paint Correction & Protection

Bring the depth back to the paint.

Sunlight reveals what ordinary lighting can hide. Paint correction is not simply adding shine. It starts with evaluating the paint, testing a suitable polishing approach and refining the surface as safely as the paint condition permits.

Stage 01

Decontaminate

Remove surface contamination using suitable wash, chemical and mechanical decontamination methods.

Stage 02

Inspect & Test

Assess defects and paint condition. Test a small area before choosing the correction method.

Stage 03

Correct & Refine

Use an appropriate combination of compounds and polishes to improve clarity while managing paint removal.

Stage 04

Protect

Prepare the surface and apply the selected sealant or ceramic coating according to curing requirements.
