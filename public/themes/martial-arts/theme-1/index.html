The Journey Of A Lifetime

Belt Progression & Student Development

A black belt is simply a white belt who never gave up. Understand the milestones on your martial arts path.

White Belt

Months 0 – 18

Survival, fundamental postures, escapes, and subduing the ego. Learning the language of the mats.

Blue Belt

Years 2 – 4

Technical fluency, guard retention, offensive passing systems, and defensive composure under pressure.

Purple Belt

Years 4 – 7

Developing your individual game, deceptive chain attacks, and guiding lower belts with technical insight.

Brown Belt

Years 7 – 9

Mastering timing, micro-adjustments, fluid submission finishes, and refining counter-attacks.

Black Belt

Years 10+

A new beginning. Humility, complete technical mastery, leadership, and embodying the martial way of life.
