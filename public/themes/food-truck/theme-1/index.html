THE ORIGIN

IT STARTED WITH A HOT GRIDDLE AND A BIG IDEA.

We didn't set out to make another food truck. We wanted to make the kind of food people talk about on the drive home.

It began with late-night burger experiments, a griddle running hot, and the search for that perfect combination: crispy beef edges, melted cheese, a soft toasted bun, and sauce that brings everything together.
