Expandable Job Summary & Quick Facts
Concrete pavers prepare subgrades, build formwork, place and consolidate wet concrete, level slabs to laser grade, and apply durable surface finishes across roads, sidewalks, driveways, and industrial floors.
03 · What You Actually Do

Six Core Responsibilities

01

Ground Preparation

Excavating soil, compacting subbase aggregate, and checking drainage slopes.

02

Formwork Setup

Anchoring edge forms and placing steel reinforcement where specified.

03

Concrete Mixing

Inspecting slump consistency and coordinating batch delivery timing.

04

Pouring & Vibrating

Spreading wet mix evenly and eliminating air pockets with vibrators.

05

Leveling & Finishing

Screeding to grade, bull floating, edging, and power troweling.

06

Jointing & Curing

Sawing control joints and protecting hydration during curing.
