02 · Definition & Comparison

The Meaning of Concrete Paving

Concrete paving is the civil construction trade of forming, placing, consolidating, and finishing Portland cement concrete slabs into rigid load-bearing surfaces.

PropertyConcrete Paving (Rigid)Asphalt Paving (Flexible)
Load DistributionBridges across wide slab areaTransfers load directly to base
Service Lifespan30–50 years with joint care15–20 years with resurfacing
Heat ReflectionHigh albedo, cooler surfaceDark surface absorbs heat

05 · Material Science

Cement, Aggregates & Equipment

Portland Cement

Hydraulic binder reacting with water through hydration to harden the matrix.

Fine & Coarse Aggregates

Washed sand and crushed stone providing compressive strength and volume stability.

Water & Equipment

Controlled water-cement ratio placed via mixers, vibrators, screeds, and trowels.

07 · Skills & Safety

Craftsmanship & PPE

Requires laser measurement accuracy, timing of float/trowel passes, teamwork during pours, and strict PPE (hard hat, alkali-resistant boots/gloves, silica control).

Searchable Glossary

Concrete Terminology

Slump: Measure of wet concrete consistency and workability.
Screed: Tool or machine used to strike off concrete to grade.
Curing: Maintaining moisture and temperature for strength gain.
Control Joint: Tooled or sawed groove controlling shrinkage cracks.
