General Plumbing Issues

Why is my water pressure suddenly so low?
Sudden low water pressure can be caused by several issues. It could be a closed or partially closed shut-off valve, a failing pressure regulator, mineral buildup in the pipes, or a hidden water leak. If the issue is affecting the whole house, we highly recommend scheduling an inspection to rule out major pipe leaks.
What should I NEVER put down my garbage disposal?
To avoid serious clogs, never put the following items down your disposal: liquid grease or oil (it solidifies in pipes), coffee grounds, eggshells, fibrous vegetables (like celery or onion skins), pasta, rice, and bones. Always run cold water while operating the disposal.
How do I know if I have a hidden water leak?
Common signs of a hidden leak include: an unusually high water bill, the sound of running water when all fixtures are turned off, damp or warm spots on your floors, mold or mildew smells, and cracks in your foundation. We offer non-invasive acoustic leak detection to find the exact source.

Water Heaters

How long does a standard water heater last?
A traditional tank-style water heater typically lasts between 8 to 12 years with proper maintenance. Tankless water heaters have a longer lifespan, often lasting 15 to 20 years. If your heater is over 10 years old and requiring frequent repairs, replacement is usually the most cost-effective option.
Why is my water heater making a popping or rumbling noise?
Popping or rumbling noises are usually caused by hard water mineral sediment building up at the bottom of the tank. When the burner heats the tank, water trapped under the sediment boils and bursts through, causing the sound. Flushing the tank annually can prevent this.
