Good questions deserve clear answers.

What happens during a comprehensive eye examination? +

The examination depends on your individual needs and may include discussing your visual history, assessing vision and checking relevant aspects of eye health. Your optometrist will explain which tests are appropriate.

What is digital retinal imaging? +

It is a non-invasive scan that takes high-resolution images of the back of your eye. This helps our optometrists detect and monitor conditions that might not be visible during a standard exam.

Can I buy glasses without an eye examination? +

Yes, if you have a valid, in-date prescription from another qualified professional, you can absolutely bring it in and our styling team will help you find the perfect frames.

Do you offer prescription sunglasses? +

Yes! The majority of our optical frames and dedicated sunglasses collection can be fitted with prescription sun lenses, complete with UV protection.
